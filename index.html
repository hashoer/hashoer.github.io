About Us

Hashoer Intelligent Technology (Shanghai) Ltd.

A professional manufacturer of non-standard packaging equipment, Hashoer specializes in pharmaceutical machinery, medical devices, and laboratory solutions. Since our founding, we've been guided by market demand, pursuing the spirit of Professionalism · Integrity · Excellence.

Backed by advanced technology from Europe, we bring together a team of packaging engineering specialists serving the pharmaceutical, daily chemical, and food industries — from standalone machines to turnkey automated production lines.

Our team includes four mid-level engineers and 500 skilled technicians. Our field engineers supplement your maintenance operations, optimize equipment efficiency, and deliver turnkey solutions for your quality, production, and business challenges.
