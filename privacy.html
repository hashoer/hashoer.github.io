2. Information we collect

When you submit our enquiry form or contact us through WhatsApp or email, we receive the information you choose to provide. Our form asks for:

Like most websites, we automatically receive technical information when you browse, including:

  • your IP address (used to estimate your approximate location — country and city, not a precise address)

3. Why we use this information

  • to answer your enquiry and provide quotations or technical information
  • to understand how our website is used, so we can improve its content and structure
  • to understand which countries, markets and product pages attract interest

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies for website analytics. Our analytics provider sets identifiers such as _ga and _ga_*, which distinguish one browser from another and may remain on your device for up to two years. We use this data only in aggregate form to understand traffic patterns.

You can refuse or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings, and you can install Google's official Analytics opt-out browser add-on. If you block cookies, the website will still work; only statistical measurement is affected.
